# Cross-Platform Marketing Playbook

**Golden rule (from `docs/go-to-market.md`):** every channel serves the **ground
game**. Online is **credibility + reach + something to bring to the in-person
conversation** — not the closer. One handle everywhere: **@dinerohwy**. Every
profile links to **dinerohwy.com** (or `/galveston` for the local push).

## Channels & jobs

### X / Twitter — @dinerohwy · "real developer" credibility
- **Job:** build-in-public. Prove Dinero Hwy is a real builder, not a template mill.
- **Post:** the tools shipping (quote demo, review app, the Get-Online ad), the
  fromgalveston.com Island Breeze aggregator, before/after of a client site, GitHub snippets.
- **Audience:** devs, tech-curious, local founders. Warms credibility you cash in
  on the ground.

### Facebook — the local money channel for THIS audience
- **Job:** reach the 45+ owners who actually live on Facebook, via **local groups**
  (Galveston community/business/buy-sell-trade groups, chambers).
- **Post:** the free Report Card offer, client wins, the 35-sec Get-Online video
  (captioned, works muted), simple "did you know 8.9M people visit Galveston?"
  value posts. This is the **1×1×1 channel** (one avatar × Report Card × FB group).

### Instagram — visual proof + local flavor
- **Job:** before/after reels, the Get-Online ad, Galveston shots, the Island Breeze aggregator.
- Link-in-bio → `/galveston` / Report Card.

### The site — the hub
- Everything points home. `/galveston` is the local landing; `/free` is the magnet;
  the Report Card is the offer.

### YouTube (light) — evergreen
- The Get-Online ad + a short "how a Galveston shop got found" once there's a case.

## Content pillars (rotate)
1. **Build-in-public** (X-first): what shipped, how, the AI stack.
2. **Local value** (FB/IG-first): Galveston facts, the Island Breeze aggregator, "found online" tips.
3. **Proof**: before/afters, client wins, live demos.
4. **Education**: the "Agentic AI like a kid" book — approachable AI content.

## Cadence (bootstrapped, sustainable)
- **X:** 3–5×/week build-in-public.
- **FB (incl. groups):** 2–3×/week; the Report Card offer weekly.
- **IG:** 2×/week (repurpose X/FB).
- Batch on one day; schedule. Consistency > volume.

## Swipe copy (starting points — keep the ethos: opportunity, dignity, no fear)
- **FB group / offer:** "Galveston gets 8.9M visitors a year. Are they finding your
  spot online? I'll grade your website & Google presence free and send 3 fixes —
  no cost, no pitch. Local, built by hand. Comment 'card' or DM."
- **X build-in-public:** "Shipped a tool today: a customer snaps a photo of the job
  and gets an instant quote emailed back. Built on Cloudflare with Claude/ChatGPT/
  Grok. Runs live on the site — try it. 🚦"
- **IG reel caption:** "Your business deserves to be found. Here's a Galveston
  shop, online in a weekend. Scan the sticker around town → free Report Card."

## Measurement
- Link-in-bio and posts use UTMs (`utm_source=x|facebook|instagram`) → land on
  `/galveston` or `/free` → tagged in D1 (`EmailCapture` source). Review weekly:
  which platform actually produced captures / conversations.
- **Reality check:** expect the *conversations* (not the posts) to close. Social
  warms; you close in person.
