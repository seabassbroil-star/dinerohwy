Wider net: from hidden meaning to minted metal

Rounds 1–2 (monograms, screens-and-coins) live in version history. This round deliberately spans four opposite corners — abstract-with-a-secret, bold fintech glyph, pure growth gesture, and crafted emblem — so your reaction tells us the direction even if no single mark is final yet. Each card notes which corner it's from.

01 · Six marks

Round 3 concepts

R1 · The growth d
Corner: hidden in plain sight
Customer readAt first: a bold green "d" for dinero. Then: the arrow pointing up inside it.

FedEx-style double read — the negative space of the d is an upward arrow. Money conveyed by growth, no $ anywhere.

16px — d + arrow survive
R2 · The đ — a minted currency symbol
Corner: hidden in plain sight / craft
Customer read"That looks like a currency sign… for their own money."

Currency symbols are letters with strike-bars ($, €, ¥, ₿). This invents one for dinero: a d with double bars. Ownable, classy, and the brand story writes itself — "we mint new money for local businesses."

16px — đ survives
R3 · Steps up
Corner: bold fintech glyph
Customer read"Their thing makes numbers go up — and there's the money at the top."

Cash-App-energy badge: three rising bars, a coin cresting the tallest. Punchy, unmistakable, great avatar.

16px — bars + coin survive
R4 · The lift
Corner: abstract gesture
Customer read"Up and to the right." The universal shape of doing well.

One confident stroke rising to a coin — a swoosh that means revenue. The most abstract of the set; lives or dies on execution polish.

16px — stroke + coin survive
R5 · The mint
Corner: crafted emblem
Customer read"This looks like actual currency — established, trustworthy."

The đ glyph struck onto a coin face: gold rim, reeded (dashed) inner edge. Feels like an institution rather than an app; strong on invoices, stamps, wax-seal-style watermarks.

16px — coin + glyph survive
R6 · The internet inside the d
Corner: hidden in plain sight
Customer readA "d" whose window is open for business — the internet living inside dinero.

Inverts round 2: instead of money on a screen, the screen is inside the money-letter. Subtle at logo size, disappears gracefully to a plain bold d at 16 px.

16px — reads as bold d
